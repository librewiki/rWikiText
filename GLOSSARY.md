# Wiki document conversion

rWikiText's document-conversion domain includes MediaWiki wikitext, NamuMark, GitHub Flavored Markdown (GFM), and HTML.

## Language

**MediaWiki wikitext**

The wiki markup language interpreted by MediaWiki. In this project, the unqualified term "wikitext" refers to MediaWiki wikitext.

**NamuMark (나무마크)**

The wiki markup language used by NamuWiki, distinct from MediaWiki wikitext.

**Document meaning**

The rendered content and structure of a document, together with its page metadata.

**Page metadata**

Information that affects a wiki page beyond its rendered body, such as category membership, the redirect target, or the display title.

**Wiki context**

The current page and site settings that determine how wikitext is interpreted, including the page title, site language, and reference time.

**Transclusion**

Inclusion of content from another wiki page, often a template evaluated with arguments supplied at the point of inclusion.

**Conversion loss**

A part of the document meaning that the converted document cannot carry in its output format.
